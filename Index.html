FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions about our services.

How long does it take to build a website?

Timeline depends on complexity. Landing pages take 3-7 days, business websites 1-4 weeks, and complex systems 4-16 weeks. We provide detailed timelines during consultation.

What is your payment process?

We require 50% downpayment to begin work, with the remaining 50% due upon project completion and your approval. We offer milestone-based payments for larger projects.

Do you offer maintenance after launch?

Yes! We offer ongoing maintenance and support packages. Bug fixes are included, and we provide performance monitoring and updates as needed.

How do you ensure security?

Security is built into every project from day one. We implement RBAC, encryption, input validation, anti-injection measures, and conduct thorough security testing before launch.

Can you work with my existing system?

Absolutely! We can integrate with existing systems, migrate data, or enhance current applications. We'll assess your current setup during consultation.

What technologies do you specialize in?

We specialize in Laravel, React, PHP, MySQL, JavaScript, and modern CSS frameworks. We choose the best technology based on your specific project needs.
