📋 Faculty Evaluation System · Full Case Study

Palompon Institute of Technology – Tabango Campus | CHED CMO No. 19, s. 2025

🎯 Problem Statement

Manual paper‑based evaluations caused delays, clerical errors, and difficulty in aggregating feedback. Data redundancy and lack of real‑time reports hindered decision‑making.

💡 Solution

Automated online platform with role‑based access. Real‑time dashboards, automated computation of weighted means, and encrypted data storage ensure reliability and CHED compliance.

🛠️ Tech Stack

PHP · MySQL · JavaScript · Bootstrap · XAMPP · Chart.js
